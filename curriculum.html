Black Friday Command
The Curriculum

How to run your whole Black Friday sale with the Black Friday Command Generator — your offers, your prices, your shop. Fill in the gold boxes with YOUR business.

How This Works

1 · Set up once

Put your product, prices, dates and shop link into the Generator one time. Every tool pulls from that same setup.

2 · Generate everything

One tap per asset: commercial, video ad, flyer, ad copy, captions, pin, email, live script.

3 · Work the 7 days

Follow the Runway Plan at the end of this curriculum so the right asset goes out on the right day.

You need: the Generator open, your shop link, your product prices, and one AI chat (ChatGPT or Claude) for the Master Prompt below. That's it.

Paste this into a fresh ChatGPT or Claude chat FIRST. It turns your AI into your Black Friday marketing director for every lesson that follows. Replace every [BRACKET] with your own details.

MY BUSINESS:

🛠 Steps

Lesson 1 · Price It Like a Marketer

🎯 What you'll learn

The price is a message. Your Black Friday price should feel like a door opening, not a fire sale at a closing store.

🛠 The rules

  • Anchor high, land fair: show the regular price next to the sale price every time. The gap is the story.
  • Charm the number: prices ending in 7 or 9 ($97, $27) outsell round numbers in tests, year after year.
  • Protect your regulars: your full-price buyers should feel smart, not punished — frame Black Friday as a founder's thank-you, once a year.
  • Never fake it: a "discount" off a price you never charged is illegal in many places and always brand-poison.

✍️ Fill in yours

My anchor (regular) price: $[___]
My Black Friday price: $[___]
The result my buyer gets for that price, in one sentence: [___]
💡 Pro tip: If your margin allows one extra, add a bonus instead of cutting deeper. Bonuses raise value; deeper cuts train people to wait for sales.
Lesson 2 · Your Commercial

🎯 What you'll make

A 27-second commercial script with a shot list — hook, problem, fix, proof, offer, end card.

🛠 Steps

  1. In the Generator, pick Commercial Script and hit Generate.
  2. Read the hook out loud. If you wouldn't stop scrolling for it, hit "Give me another version."
  3. Film it with your phone, your avatar, or your animation tool — one shot at a time, exactly as listed.

A copy-paste video ad prompt for your AI video/image tool, plus flyer copy and a matching design prompt for your graphics.

🛠 Steps

  1. Pick Video Ad Prompt, generate, and paste the whole block into your video tool. Keep the on-screen text exactly as written — words are locked.
  2. Pick Flyer + Design Prompt. Paste the copy into your design app and run the design prompt for the background/hero image.
  3. Save three versions: one for social (vertical), one for email (wide), one for your shop banner.

Lesson 4 · Ad Copy & Captions

🎯 What you'll make

Paid ad copy (two versions plus headline) and organic captions for TikTok/Reels, Facebook, and Threads — same offer, each platform's native voice.

🛠 Steps

  1. Generate Ad Copy (FB/IG). Version A tells the story; Version B is the punchy retargeting version for people who already saw you once.
  2. Generate Social Captions and schedule one per platform per day of your sale — never copy-paste the identical text everywhere.
  3. End every caption with one clear action: tap the link, comment the word, or save the post.

✍️ Fill in yours

My hashtags (5–8, niche not noisy): [___]
💡 Pro tip: Talk like your buyer talks to herself at midnight. Mirror her words — "I keep starting over," "I don't have time" — then answer them.
Lesson 5 · Pinterest & Email — The Quiet Money

🎯 What you'll make

A keyword-rich Pinterest pin that keeps selling after the sale week, and an email blast with three subject lines.

🛠 Steps

  1. Generate the Pinterest Pin. Post it the day your doors open; pins peak days later, so it lands mid-sale.
  2. Generate the Email Blast. Send #1 at doors-open, and a shorter "last call" the final day using subject line 3.
  3. Your email list is the only audience you own. Every sale, invite buyers onto it.

✍️ Fill in yours

My email send times: doors-open [___] · last call [___]
💡 Pro tip: Subject lines are headlines for the inbox. Curiosity + deadline wins: "[END DATE] it goes back to full price…" beats "Newsletter #42" every time.
Lesson 6 · Go Live & Sell

🎯 What you'll make

A full live-selling script: the 60-second opening, an 8-minute rotation that re-sells every new joiner, and a closing that respects the deadline.

🛠 Steps

  1. Generate the Live Selling Script and keep it beside you — it's a loop, not a lecture. Every rotation: story, demo, offer, objections, call to action.
  2. Pin your shop link as the first comment before you start.
  3. Demo beats description. Show the thing working, live, at least once per rotation.
  4. Name your buyers out loud when they grab it. Celebration is social proof.

✍️ Fill in yours

My "type this when you buy" word: [GOT IT / I'M IN / ___]
💡 Pro tip: New people join every minute and know nothing. Never say "as I said earlier" — just run the loop again like it's the first time.
Lesson 7 · The Story Commercial

🎯 What you'll make

A 90-second character commercial in the classic holiday shape: a grump who won't buy in, a guide who shows her the way, three stops where your product solves her exact problem, and a joke at the end she'll repeat for you.

🛠 Steps

  1. Pick Story Commercial in the Generator. It writes the full structure with your product in all three stops.
  2. Cast for contrast: grump vs. guide. The comedy — and the selling — lives in the chemistry.
  3. Color tells the story: cold and grey before, warmer at every stop, gold at the win.
  4. Steal the shape, never the characters: classic tales are for everyone, but your grump and your guide must be YOUR originals.

✍️ Fill in yours

My guide (who/what shows her the way): [___]
Her three problems → my product's three answers: 1) [___] → [___] 2) [___] → [___] 3) [___] → [___]
My final joke: [___]
💡 Pro tip: The grump never becomes sweet — she becomes convinced. Let her keep her attitude and change her mind; audiences trust that more.

🗓️ Your 7-Day Runway — Final Checklist

Run the Generator's 7-Day Content Plan, then check off your sale week. (Dates shown as your sale's own rhythm — shift to match your [START DATE] – [END DATE].)

  • Doors Open Day: commercial live AM · email #1 sent · pin posted
  • Proof Day: demo/result video posted · story poll running
  • Big Day: LIVE with the selling script — midday and an evening encore
  • Objection Day: answer the top 3 questions as a post or reel
  • Proof Day 2: wins, screenshots, shoutouts · second pin, fresh title
  • Last Full Day: "tomorrow it's gone" asset · last-call email loaded
  • Final Day: final-call post AM · last-chance LIVE evening · thank-you post tomorrow
💡 The marketer's secret: most sales land on the first day and the last day. Your middle days exist to give people reasons — proof, answers, and other people's wins. Work the plan and let the deadline close for you.
